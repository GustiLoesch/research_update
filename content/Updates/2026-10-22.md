# Progress update — 22 October 2026

## Main takeaway

This first shared update brings together the priorities recorded in the project notes. HoB₄ is the main materials focus; Virtual TOPAS needs a reproducible baseline; LuFeO₃ needs a clearer measurement strategy. The next discussion should turn these directions into agreed near-term outputs and a realistic allocation of time.

This is a starting overview, rather than a record of new experimental or computational results completed this week.

## Progress and evidence available

- **[[HoB4|HoB₄]]:** The September strategy records the focus decision and proposes using existing data while preparing crystal growth.
