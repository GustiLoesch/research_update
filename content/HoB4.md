# HoB₄

[[Planning/Shared/index|Research progress]]

**Role:** Main materials project, following the focus decision recorded on 10 September 2026.

## Research question

The immediate task is to select a focused question about magnetic order or excitations in HoB₄ that can be addressed with available data and a realistic measurement plan.

Candidate directions include quantitative modelling of magnetic excitations, the dynamics associated with incommensurate order, and field-induced magnetic phases. These are alternatives to assess, rather than an agreed programme of measurements.

## Approach

- Review the existing literature and the measurements discussed in Daniel Brunt's thesis.
- Establish access to a suitable existing neutron dataset and assess what can be learned from it.
- Prepare crystal growth in parallel, including materials, training, preparation steps, and characterization.
- Use the initial analysis to identify the need for further measurements.

## Current position

The project notes record HoB₄ as the preferred initial focus and contain a survey of previous facility measurements, growth preparation notes, and possible research questions. Dataset access, sample availability, and a final analysis target remain open in those notes. A completed growth run or new analysis result is not documented in this shared overview.
