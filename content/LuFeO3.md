# LuFeO₃

[[index|Research progress]]

**Current focus:** Clarify the experimental strategy and whether the target magnetic signal can be resolved.

## Research question

Which neutron-scattering measurement can distinguish the proposed magnetic excitation features, with sufficient energy and momentum resolution?

The specific signal, measurement conditions, and success criterion still need to be agreed.

## Approach

- Define the target excitation, expected splitting, and relevant momentum positions.
- Assess a time-of-flight measurement before triple-axis spectroscopy with polarization analysis, as suggested in the September project notes.
- Evaluate resolution at the actual measurement positions and conditions, including broadening from momentum integration.
- Confirm instrument suitability and proposal timing with the relevant instrument scientists.

## Current position

The existing notes contain preliminary resolution estimates and possible instrument options. They record the suggestion of a time-of-flight measurement before polarized triple-axis work. These are planning inputs; an agreed configuration or confirmed beamtime is not established by this overview.

## Next proposed milestones

| Milestone | Expected output |
| --- | --- |
| Define the target signal | Energy, momentum, expected splitting, and sample or domain conditions. |
| Assess feasibility | Resolution and accessible coverage for a realistic configuration. |
| Agree the measurement sequence | A justified choice of time-of-flight and/or polarized triple-axis work. |
| Confirm the proposal route | Instrument-scientist feedback and a checked submission timetable. |

## Decisions needed

- What signal and resolution criterion should determine feasibility?
- Should time-of-flight mapping precede polarization analysis?
- What priority should this work receive relative to HoB₄ and Virtual TOPAS?
